## Exercises to produce

### Hip abductor clamshells  —  `clamshells.png`

- Specs: 3×15 (add a band above the knees once easy)
- Cue: _lie on your side, stack hips, open the top knee without rolling your pelvis back_
- Used in: IT band, Piriformis, Groin / adductor, Glute, Hip, Hip & core base

### Lateral band walks  —  `lateral_band_walks.png`

- Specs: 2×20 steps each way
- Cue: _band above the knees, stay in a half-squat, small controlled steps, keep tension_
- Used in: IT band, Piriformis, Glute, Hip, Hip & core base

### Foam roll TFL & outer glute  —  `foam_roll_tfl.png`

- Specs: 1–2 min
- Cue: _roll the muscle just below the hip bone — never the IT band itself, that irritates it_
- Used in: IT band

### Hip flexor lunge stretch  —  `hip_flexor_stretch.png`

- Specs: 3×30s each side
- Cue: _half-kneel, tuck your pelvis under, press the hips forward — don't arch the low back_

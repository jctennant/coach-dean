## IT band  —  `it_band.png`

_Builds the glute-med strength that stops the IT band overloading, and calms the lateral knee._

- **Hip abductor clamshells** — 3×15 (add a band above the knees once easy)  
  _lie on your side, stack hips, open the top knee without rolling your pelvis back_
- **Lateral band walks** — 2×20 steps each way  
  _band above the knees, stay in a half-squat, small controlled steps, keep tension_
- **Foam roll TFL & outer glute** — 1–2 min  
  _roll the muscle just below the hip bone — never the IT band itself, that irritates it_
- **Hip flexor lunge stretch** — 3×30s each side  
  _half-kneel, tuck your pelvis under, press the hips forward — don't arch the low back_

## Shin splints  —  `shin.png`

_Loads the tibialis and calf so the shin can handle ground impact without flaring._

- **Eccentric calf raises (straight knee)** — 3×15  
  _off a step, rise on both feet, lower slowly on the working leg_
- **Tibialis anterior raises** — 3×15  
  _stand with your back to a wall, heels down, lift your toes toward your shins_
- **Calf stretch (straight + bent knee)** — 3×30s each  
  _back heel down against a wall, hold both the straight- and bent-knee positions_
- **Toe taps on a stair** — 2×20  
  _controlled light taps — you should feel the front of the shin working_

## Calf / Achilles  —  `calf.png`

_Eccentric calf loading is the best-evidenced rehab for Achilles and calf strain._

- **Eccentric heel drops** — 3×15 straight + 3×15 bent knee  
  _rise on two feet, lower slowly on the affected leg off a step edge_
- **Single-leg calf raises** — 3×20  
  _full range, pause at the top, controlled down_
- **Soleus stretch (bent knee)** — 3×30s  
  _back knee slightly bent, heel stays down_
- **Ankle circles** — 2×10 each direction  
  _slow, full range of motion_

## Runner's knee  —  `knee.png`

_Quad and glute strength to track the kneecap properly and offload the joint._

- **VMO quad sets** — 3×15  
  _sit with the leg straight, tighten the thigh, hold 5s, relax_
- **Terminal knee extensions (band)** — 3×15  
  _loop a band behind the knee, straighten the leg fully against it_
- **Step-downs** — 3×10 each  
  _from a 6-inch step, lower slowly over 3s, keep the knee tracking over your toes_
- **Straight-leg raises** — 3×15  
  _lie back, lock the leg straight, lift to ~45° and lower slowly_

## Hamstring  —  `hamstring.png`

_Eccentric hamstring loading to build strain resilience through full range._

- **Eccentric Nordic hamstring curls** — 3×8 (towel under knees)  
  _anchor your ankles, lower as slowly as you can, hands ready to catch you_
- **Single-leg Romanian deadlift** — 3×10 each  
  _hinge at the hip with a flat back, feel the standing-leg hamstring load_
- **Prone hamstring raises** — 3×12  
  _lie face down, bend the knee to lift your heel toward your glute, slow_
- **Glute bridges** — 3×15 (2-sec hold at top)  
  _drive through your heels, squeeze the glutes, ribs down — don't arch the back_

## Foot / plantar fascia  —  `foot.png`

_Calms the plantar fascia and rebuilds the intrinsic foot strength that supports the arch._

- **Frozen bottle arch roll** — 2 min each foot  
  _roll the arch back and forth over a frozen water bottle_
- **Towel toe curls** — 3×15  
  _scrunch a towel toward you using only your toes_
- **Eccentric calf raises (straight knee)** — 3×15  
  _off a step, rise on both feet, lower slowly on the working leg_
- **Short-foot arch activation** — 3×10 (5s holds)  
  _lift the arch without curling the toes, hold, release_

## Ankle  —  `ankle.png`

## Lower back  —  `back.png`

_Gentle mobility plus core control to settle the low back and support running posture._

- **Cat-cow** — 10 slow reps  
  _move slowly between rounding and arching, breathe with it_
- **Bird-dog** — 3×10 each side  
  _extend opposite arm + leg, keep your hips level, no rotation_
- **Child's pose** — 2×60s  
  _hips back to heels, arms long, breathe into the low back_
- **Dead bug** — 3×8 each side  
  _low back pinned to the floor, extend opposite arm + leg slowly_

## Hip & core base  —  `hip_core.png`

_The strongest general injury-prevention evidence we have (Run RCT, Leppänen 2024): hip + core strength twice a week cut overuse injuries roughly in half._

- **Side plank (hip lifts)** — 3×20–30s each  
  _elbow under the shoulder, lift the hips, add small dips once steady_
- **Single-leg squat (to a chair)** — 3×8 each  
  _sit back to a chair on one leg, knee tracks over the toes, control the lower_
- **Single-leg glute bridges** — 3×12 each  
  _one foot planted, other leg extended, keep your hips level the whole time_
- **Lateral band walks** — 2×20 steps each way  
  _band above the knees, stay in a half-squat, small controlled steps, keep tension_
- **Front plank** — 3×30–45s  
  _straight line head to heels, brace the core, don't let the hips sag_
